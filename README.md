# Morava: reading notes and visualization sketches

Notes on the mathematics of **Jack Johnson Morava (1944–2025)** and related work. This begins with the [nLab entry on Jack Morava](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/Jack+Morava), followed by the original papers and expository sources linked below.

**Status (2026-10-09):** exploratory notes, mainly based on indexed nLab entries, publication records, and paper abstracts. They are **not** claims to have checked every proof or bibliography. A visualization of an algebraic model must not be passed off as a rendering of a spectrum.

## Reading paths

1. **Chromatic homotopy:** formal group laws, height, Morava K- and E-theories, stabilizer groups, Lubin–Tate deformations, chromatic towers.
2. **Braid groups and configuration spaces:** Morava–Rolfsen on Burau representations and TQFT; a particularly concrete route to animation.
3. **Symplectic geometry and cobordism:** circle actions, Lagrangian correspondences, stable symplectic categories, and their topological refinements.
4. **Groupoids, incidence and symmetry:** stratified quotient spaces, the eight-element quaternion group, and Morava's deliberately speculative connections to other subjects.
